About

Second-year IT student, mostly self-taught outside of class.

Background

I'm studying a Bachelor of Information Technology at La Trobe University with a software engineering major. My interests sit across systems administration, networking and cybersecurity.

I work in back operations at JB Hi-Fi and as a multi-skill cinema worker at Reading Cinemas. Before that I was a technical sales specialist at Jaycar Electronics, which is where most of my hands-on troubleshooting comes from: diagnosing laptops, cameras, dashcams and 12V electrical setups for people who often couldn't describe the fault.

Why a home lab

Coursework teaches the concepts. A lab is where I break things and have to work out why. It started as a Raspberry Pi running a few self-hosted services and moved to a Proxmox host on a repurposed Surface Pro, which I have since retired. The next step is a proper rack with segmentation, a dedicated firewall and a small SIEM, closer to what a real small-business network looks like.

CSE2HUM, Human Factors in Cyber Security

Cyber threats and human exploitation in healthcare SMEs

An industry threat analysis of how cybercriminals target small healthcare providers, why these attacks succeed so often, and what practical defences small teams can afford. Key points: healthcare recorded more data breach notifications than any other sector in Australia (OAIC, 2025), attackers succeeded in 95% of the healthcare attacks they attempted (Avant, 2025), and in one recent case the 22 day delay in notifying patients drew more criticism than the breach itself.

Read the report (PDF, 7 pages)

CSE2SIA, Security and Information Assurance

Medibank 2022 data breach: report and slides

A group case study on how the 2022 Medibank breach happened, what risks it created and which controls would have reduced them. Done with Javier Diaz-Aliste and Eddie Gosden. My parts were the introduction, the attack path (with each stage marked as confirmed, alleged or inferred), and two control comparisons: phishing-resistant MFA versus zero trust network access, and an in-house SIEM and SOC versus managed detection and response.

Read the group report and slides (PDF, 14 pages)

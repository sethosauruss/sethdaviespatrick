SaveState

In progress

A game logging app in the spirit of Letterboxd, for games instead of films.

The idea

  • Log the games you've finished
  • Track level or chapter progress
  • Write reviews
  • Link to walkthroughs
  • Run on both mobile and desktop

It's in the early stages. This page will get a proper write-up once there is something worth showing.
